Merry Mini-Games — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026
Merry Mini-Games does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Overview

Merry Mini-Games (“the app”) is a collection of festive mini-games published by Epic Growth Solutions. We designed it to respect your privacy completely: the app works entirely on your device and does not send any information about you anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or usage data. We have no servers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best scores, daily challenge results, advent calendar progress and your sound and haptics settings locally on your device (using the operating system’s standard on-device storage), so they persist between sessions. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is permanently removed if you delete the app.

Network use

The app does not require an internet connection and does not make network requests to us or to any third party during normal play.

Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and post the revised version at this address.
