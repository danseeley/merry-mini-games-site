Common questions

Where are my scores kept? On your device, and nowhere else. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded, so scores don't follow you to a new phone.

Does it need the internet? No. The whole app works offline, including the daily challenge — the date itself is the shared seed, so everyone gets the same board without anything being synced.

Why is the advent calendar locked? Doors open on their own day, December 1st to 25th. Outside December the calendar shows as a preview.

How do I turn off music, sound or haptics? Tap the ⓘ button on the home screen.
