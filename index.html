Christmas, one round at a time.

Merry Mini-Games is a cosy collection of seven festive mini-games wrapped in hand-sculpted clay art and gentle falling snow. A new shared challenge every day, 25 advent doors to open, and medals to chase — all of it offline, and none of it watching you.

Seven games in one app

Quick arcade rounds when you have a minute, slower puzzles when you have longer. Every one of them keeps its own best score and its own set of medals. Every clip below is real gameplay, recorded on an iPhone.

Arcade

Gift Catch

Drag your sleigh to catch the falling gifts, grab the glowing golden bonus and dodge the lumps of coal. Build combos for big scores before the level timer runs out.

Puzzle

Memory Match

Flip the cards to find matching pairs of Santa, snowmen, reindeer and more. Fewer moves and a faster time mean a higher score.

Arcade

Whack-an-Elf

Bonk the elves and Santa as they pop out of the snow — but never the Grinch. Thirty seconds on the clock and no time to think.

Physics puzzle

Merry Merge

A relaxing drop-and-combine puzzle. Stack ornaments in the jar and merge matching ones to climb the ladder — cranberry, bell, gingerbread, all the way up to the Christmas tree.

Puzzle

Festive Slide

Slide the tiles to put a festive scene back together — a snowy village, a workshop bench, a lit tree in a window. Tap a tile or drag it, on a 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5 board, with a press-and-hold peek for when you lose the thread.

Word

Word Wreath

Find the hidden festive words in the grid — forwards, backwards and on every diagonal. Three grid sizes, six themed word pools, and every word you find is ringed in its own colour so crossing words stay clear.

Puzzle

Globe Sort

Pour coloured sand between snow globes until each globe holds a single colour — six, nine or twelve, as you like. Finish one and it shakes, sparkles and keeps a quiet glow. Every deal can be finished.

Every single day

One board. Everybody. Same day.

Every day brings a single shared Daily Challenge — the same game and the exact same board for everyone, everywhere. It is generated from the date itself, so nothing has to be downloaded or synced for your board to match your family's on the other side of the world. Keep the streak alive and share how you did.

  • 25 days of advent. Open a door a day through December for a festive fact and a sticker for your share card. No door opens early.
  • Medals. Bronze, silver and gold in every game, so there is always a next rung.
  • A home screen widget counting the sleeps 'til Christmas, with your streak and advent progress.

Support

Something not working, or just want to tell us something? Email support@epicgrowthsolutions.net and we will get back to you. It helps to include your device model and iOS version.

Where are my scores kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded, so your scores and streak do not follow you to a new phone.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The whole app works offline, the Daily Challenge included — the date itself is the shared seed, so everyone gets the same board without anything being synced.

Why is the advent calendar locked?

Doors open on their own day, December 1st to 25th. Outside December the calendar shows as a preview.

How do I turn off the music, sound or haptics?

Tap the ⓘ button in the top right of the home screen. Music, sound effects, haptics and a Large Text setting all live there.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Neither. The app is free, there is nothing to buy inside it, and there are no adverts of any kind.

What does the app collect about me?

Nothing at all — no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The full policy is on the privacy page.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. iPhone plays in portrait; iPad rotates to all four orientations.
